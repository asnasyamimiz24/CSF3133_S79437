Popular Destinations

Redang Island

Best for luxury resorts and crystal-clear turquoise waters. Redang features stunning, high-end beachfront resorts, powdery white sand, and vibrant coral reefs perfect for diving right off the beach

RM 350 RM 250 per person

Perhentian Island

Best for budget-friendly beach vibes and marine life. Divided into Perhentian Kecil (popular with backpackers for its nightlife and fire shows at Long Beach) and Perhentian Besar (quieter, ideal for families and couples). The snorkeling here gives you a very high chance of swimming with sea turtles and harmless reef sharks.

RM 320 RM 220 per person

Kenyir Lake

Kenyir Lake is the largest man-made lake in Southeast Asia, located in Terengganu, Malaysia. It is a premier eco-tourism destination surrounded by a 130-million-year-old tropical rainforest. Discover a peaceful lake surrounded by green forests and waterfalls.

RM 280 RM 199 per person
