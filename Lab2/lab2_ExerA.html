About the Event

The UMT Software Engineering Workshop is a one-day event designed to introduce students to web development and software projects.

Introduction to Web Development

Students will learn the basic structure of HTML and how to create a simple webpage.

Mini Project Challenge

Students will work together in teams to plan and present a software project idea.

Our Speakers of the Day

Dr. Asna

Dr. Asna will share knowledge about software engineering and technology.

Ms. Syamimi

Ms. Syamimi will share her knowledge about web development.
